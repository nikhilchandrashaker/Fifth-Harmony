Built from Wikipedia’s Fifth Harmony discography and tour articles, with press reports (Billboard, Stereogum, Complex, Yahoo Entertainment and others) for the 2012 formation, the hiatus and the 2025 and 2026 events. The source’s counts mostly match its tables: 12 singles including one as featured artist, 11 promotional singles (Noche de Paz is listed in a separate table and added here), 6 EPs and 17 videos. Where the article text and a table differ on certifications (Worth It, Work from Home), the table is followed. Some dates are only a month or a year, and the lineup for year-only releases follows the year. Birth dates and hometowns were not retrieved, so they are left out. Comeback reports are labelled as reported or teaser-only. No streaming data is included.
